## Predetermined overlap rule (before transcription)
1. Whole-phage genome: cluster train and candidate test with VIRIDIC at its **published default** species 95% and genus 70% intergenomic similarity thresholds. Any candidate in a training-connected genus cluster (>=70% intergenomic similarity, transitive connected component) is excluded from external scoring. The standard and caution that these are taxonomic rather than host-range thresholds are from Moraru, Varsani & Kropinski, *Viruses* 2020 [1]. Zero exact SHA256 matches is insufficient. When the tool cannot score a sequence, quarantine it; do not assume independence.
2. Receptor-binding protein: compare all detected test RBPs (including candidates whose annotations are uncertain) against every training RBP. Exclude a test phage if any protein has BLASTP E <= 0.001, >=60% query coverage, and query length >=60% subject length, the published PhaMMseqs phamily false-negative search screen [2]. This is a deliberately conservative cross-family exclusion, not proof of equal host range. Failure to find an annotated RBP is unknown, not a pass.
3. Host: exclude exact strain aliases/genome identifiers. Exclude test hosts if K-locus sequence is identical to a training host K-locus or if the entire typed K-locus has >=95% nucleotide identity across >=80% of both query and subject length. The 95%/80% rule is an explicit conservative operational threshold anchored to a published virus population overlap convention [1]; it is **not** a validated bacterial K-locus or host-range standard. Therefore any sensitivity to host cutoff, ambiguous locus assembly, or shared KL label is reported, and a shared KL label alone is flagged as a mechanism-generalization risk even when sequence falls below threshold. An independent reviewer must approve this host-rule rationale before labels are transcribed; if not, stop and version another amendment while keeping this candidate exploratory.
4. All codes, versions, metadata rows, and exclusions must be exported before labels. No threshold may change based on observed Townsend outcome patterns. If fewer than one resistant K. pneumoniae host has >=5 tested eligible lytic phages and >=1 plaque-positive outcome after exclusions, the locked hit-rate@5 endpoint is unevaluable. Do not substitute a different matrix or denominator.

The experiment remains computational; no phage engineering or wet-lab work is authorized by this protocol. A second genuinely unseen external cohort is sought in parallel. No ranking win or safety clearance is claimed at this stage.
