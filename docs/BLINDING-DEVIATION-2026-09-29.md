# Blinding deviation (2026-09-29)

The v0.1/v0.1.1 prereg said the homology exclusion thresholds and tie-breakers would be fixed before held-out labels were accessed. During the metadata audit, the analyst opened Townsend et al. Figure 3 to determine whether its outcome categories (plaques, lawn clearing, no effect) could support the endpoint. The colored outcome matrix was visually accessible at that moment; no cell-level labels were transcribed into files, no model was fit to them, and no result score was calculated. However, full blinding cannot be asserted. This deviation is recorded before any benchmark run and does not rewrite the frozen protocol.

The analytic fix is not to choose audit thresholds after viewing the matrix. For a confirmatory external claim, use a new genuinely unseen cohort or have an independent analyst freeze externally sourced identity thresholds and deduplication code without the matrix and document the residual exposure. Townsend should be treated as exploratory unless a valid independent blindness and leakage assessment is established. Do not report a win against PhageHostLearn on Townsend as if the original lock were upheld.

Sources: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8006926/ (Figure 3); private prereg versions in this repo.
