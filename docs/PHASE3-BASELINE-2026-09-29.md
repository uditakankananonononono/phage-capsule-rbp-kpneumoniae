# Phase 3: complete PhageHostLearn-style training LOGOCV, not external validation

A complete **133/133** leave-one-locus-group-out, 0.995 similarity-threshold run was executed on the public author training inputs from https://zenodo.org/records/11061100 using the author source at https://github.com/dimiboeckaerts/PhageHostLearn (`code/phagehostlearn_features.py` and `code/phagehostlearn_training.ipynb`, cells 23, 30, 31). Script `code/phagehostlearn_repro.py`, verifier `code/summarize_published_repro.py`, full pair-level predictions `code/published_repro_predictions.csv.gz`, per-host metrics `code/published_repro_perhost.csv`, and summary `code/published_repro_summary.json` are committed. All 133 fold files were present; source SHA-256s, fold names/group mapping, every 10,006 pair's author interaction label, unique pair count, 333 positives, 200 hosts and 105 phages were independently checked. Fold 0 was reproduced byte-identically. Complete predictions have a canonical CSV SHA-256 in the summary JSON. A 10,000-replicate host bootstrap, seed 20260929, provides **training-side** descriptive uncertainty only.

Among 131 author-training hosts having >=5 tested phages and >=1 positive, descriptive hit@5 was 95/131 = 0.7252 (host-bootstrap 95% CI 0.6489-0.8015) and macro per-host AUROC 0.7153 (0.6616-0.7664). All 131 AUROCs defined. Pooled pair AUROC for all 10,006 pairs across 200 hosts was 0.7513. **These are not the published paper's reported metrics, a validation score, or a superiority comparison.** The per-host denominator is a supplementary filtered training-side view rather than the author's notebook hit-ratio denominator. Its ranking and tie handling are documented in the verifier.

Runtime: Python 3.10.12, XGBoost 3.2.0, scikit-learn 1.7.2, numpy 2.2.6, pandas 2.3.3, `n_jobs=2`. The author environment was Python 3.9.7, XGBoost 1.5.0 and scikit-learn 0.24.2, with `n_jobs=6`, unspecified model seed, and unordered `set()` enumeration of phages. This run uses sorted phages and random seed 20260929. Thus this is a **complete author-parameter reimplementation, not a bit-exact reproduction of the published runtime or a recovered author model**. Features and hyperparameters follow the author's locus-then-mean-RBP order, per-fold positive weighting, learning rate .3, 250 trees and depth 7. The separate prototype used RBP-then-locus, host-wise rather than locus-group held-out folds, 60 trees at depth 4 and other hyperparameters. The two training-side values cannot be interpreted as a paired external improvement or superiority finding. No independent outcome cohort is eligible for scoring under the frozen gates: Townsend is outcome-exposed and underpowered; Ghatbale fails the RBP homology exclusion. There is **no external paired score, no efficacy claim, no safe phage designation, and no candidate nomination**.
