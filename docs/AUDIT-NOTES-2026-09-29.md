# Audit notes (2026-09-29; not a prereg amendment)

- PhageHostLearn Zenodo 11061100's RBPbase has 274 RBP records/105 phage IDs, and Locibase has 200 host IDs. Townsend 2021 Table 2 lists 30 phages: 26 lytic, 4 temperate. No exact phage name or host-ID string match. This metadata fact does not establish biological independence.
- Townsend Table 2's 30 project IDs have all been resolved to ENA sequence accession IDs using the ENA portal API, saved as `audit/townsend-accessions.json` with source descriptions. All 30 FASTAs were retrieved and hashed after bounded retries. None of the 30 has an exact genome SHA256 match to the 105 genomes in PhageHostLearn v2. Hash equality is not a homology analysis, so leave the external gate blocked pending RBP-family, genome-neighbor and host-K-locus overlap.
- Four K. pneumoniae strains in Townsend Table 1 can be matched by exact NCTC number to the culture collection's carbapenemase reference strain listing: 13439, 13440 (VIM-1), 13442 (OXA-48), 13443 (NDM-1). Carbapenemase listed is evidence of resistance mechanism, but susceptibility phenotype needs a direct match when claiming phenotypic resistance.
- The original Townsend infection matrix is Figure 3 (colored image); categories distinguish plaque formation from clearance/no effect. Do not score a mere lawn-clearing spot as productive infection. Figure interpretation must be independently checked against original pixels. No outcome labels are in the current audit artifacts.
- The 2025 paper's Supplementary Data 2-6 workbook contains per-phage depolymerase KL targets but does not replace the full 30x24 strain-level Townsend matrix. Two other infection matrices in that paper (Beamud, Ferriol-Gonzalez) overlap PhageHostLearn sources and remain barred from external evaluation.

## RBP homology screen result: Ghatbale cohort FULLY EXCLUDED (added 01:15 IST)

MMseqs2 (protein and nucleotide) is environment-blocked (prefilter index cannot fit ~700MB available RAM, all split limits fail). Documented engine substitution: parasail Smith-Waterman (BLOSUM62, gap 10/1, full traceback) for all 46 extracted Ghatbale RBP-candidate proteins x 274 training RBPs; conservative flag = pident>=30% AND qcov>=60% AND qlen>=60% tlen (SW substitute for the frozen BLASTP E<=0.001 standard; at 40-99% identity full-length, BLASTP E would be far below 0.001). Deviation disclosed: engine and E-value proxy differ from frozen MMseqs2 wording in v0.2.0; result needs independent confirmation (e.g., NCBI BLASTP) before final declaration.

RESULT: 39 flagged pairs; ALL SEVEN previously-clean phages (APV, Ace, LK1, Beam, KL35, Rec, Turmeric) carry tail-fiber RBPs with 40-99.6% identity, high-coverage homologs among training RBPs - dominantly the K65PH164 long-tail-fiber locus (gp146-149, gp55). Examples: KL35 straight tail fiber 99.6% vs K65PH164_gp55 (verified at DNA level: 99.3% over 1347nt; coordinate parsing verified against GenBank complement(124933..126279)); Rec tail fiber protein 98.9% vs gp147; KL35 long tail fiber proximal subunit 96.6% vs gp146. Note the genome-wide MinHash screen (sketch 2000, k=21) MISSED these: a ~1.3kb conserved island in a 165kb genome falls below sketch resolution - the RBP-level screen is what catches it, exactly why the frozen protocol has both.

CONSEQUENCE under frozen v0.1.2 rule 2 (any RBP hit -> exclude): the ENTIRE Ghatbale test cohort is excluded from external scoring. The v0.2.0 primary CRE arm (3 eligible hosts) and secondary arm (22 hosts) become unevaluable as held-out validation. Both external cohorts are now closed: Townsend (underpowered 3/1/0/0) and Ghatbale (RBP homology exclusion). This is the frozen-standards outcome: the field's public CRKP-relevant phage-host matrices overlap the PhageHostLearn training set too deeply to serve as held-out tests. The finding itself is a real result about benchmark contamination in phage-host prediction.
